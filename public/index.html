      Fair questions

      The honest fine print.

      Can this guarantee I'll pass an AI detector?

      No, and nobody honestly can. Detectors are inconsistent and produce false positives on human writing all the time. Writing Genie reduces the stylistic patterns those tools tend to flag and shows you the reasoning, so you can make the final call.

      Does it search the whole internet for plagiarism?

      Not the whole web. It checks Wikipedia, OpenAlex academic abstracts, and a curated quotation library, plus repeated passages inside your own document. A complete web index requires a paid service like Copyleaks or Turnitin.

      Where does my document go?

      Locally, the file stays on your machine. On the hosted version it is sent to Vercel Blob, processed by the serverless function, and deleted immediately after extraction. Only short quoted phrases are sent to the public Wikipedia and OpenAlex APIs for source matching.

      Will the clean-up change my meaning?

      It swaps words and reshapes sentences, so small changes in tone are possible. Read the result. If a passage is a direct quote, cite it. Don't paraphrase it into something the original author didn't say.

      What file types work?

      Word documents (.docx), PDFs with selectable text, plain text and Markdown. Scanned PDFs need OCR first. Legacy .doc files should be re-saved as .docx. Long documents are split into roughly 60,000-character parts automatically, processed one after another with a progress indicator, and merged into one final report.

      Do the downloads keep my formatting?

      Yes. The cleaned draft can be downloaded as DOCX or PDF with a styled title, subtitle, Heading 1 to 3 levels, bullet lists and quotations. Word documents use real heading styles, so the navigation pane works. Structure is taken from uploaded Word headings where possible and inferred from the text otherwise.

      Is there still a document size limit?

      Up to 3,000,000 characters and 25 MB per file on the hosted demo (12 MB locally). Large files upload directly to Vercel Blob, so the usual 4 MB serverless request limit does not apply. Documents beyond 60,000 characters are processed in parts automatically; above 1.2 million characters the sentence heatmap is skipped to stay inside serverless response limits.
